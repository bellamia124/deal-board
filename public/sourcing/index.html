How to use your private sourcing workspace

This is your space. Only you see your buy boxes and firms — walled off from the team ZenaTech board and from every other broker (including whoever else uses this page). What you do here is invisible to them, and theirs to you.

The 3 tabs

  • Board — the companies you've sourced. Filter them, work them, log activity. This is your search surface.
  • Buy Box — where you define what you're hunting for (a "mandate": verticals, states, size).
  • Guide — this page.

How leads actually get here read this

This page does not pull companies off the internet by itself. You tell Claude what to source — for example "source pressure washing, FL, $1–5M" — and Claude runs the search, scores every company for fit, and drops them onto your Board. Then you work them here. There's no "search the web" button on purpose; Claude is the engine and the throttle on cost.

Step 1 — Define a Buy Box

  1. Buy Box tab → + New buy box.
  2. Name it, choose Buy-side (finding companies for an acquirer) or Sell-side (finding owners to list).
  3. Build your verticals with + Add from taxonomy — pick an industry, then a subcategory (104 industries, 1,146 subcategories).
  4. Set states and size bands. Save.
  5. Tell Claude to source it. Use 🔎 View on board to jump straight to that box's results.

Step 2 — Work the Board

  • Pick a buy box in the top-left dropdown.
  • Filter by industry → subcategory, state, revenue, SDE/EBITDA, employees, or type a name in search.
  • Each card is one company. The score pill (0–100) is fit — higher is better.

What each card does

  • Owner, email, phone, revenue, city up top.
  • ✉ Acquisition vs ✉ Listing chips — copy a ready email (intro / value / breakup) or a call opener. One hat per owner: you're either the buyer's agent or the seller's agent for a given company, never both.
  • Log row — record Called / No answer / Left VM / Emailed. First touch moves the firm from new → contacted automatically.
  • Flags — Do-not-call / Do-not-email / Bounced.
  • Status — new → contacted → interested → meeting → LOI → closed (or passed).
  • Note — log a free-text note.

Enrichment (owner + email + phone)

Companies land with a score and firm info first. Claude adds the owner name and work email on the firms worth working — just ask ("enrich the top firms"). Direct cell numbers are pulled only when you ask, because they cost more. Reality check: not every small firm has an owner or email in the data — that's a data-coverage limit, not a missing feature.

📊 Stats

The Stats link (top of the page) shows your pipeline funnel, outreach logged, reachability, and a 30-day activity trend — built only from your own data.

Compliance — non-negotiable

  • Calls are human click-to-dial only. A Do-not-call flagged number is never auto-dialed.
  • Every email carries a CAN-SPAM footer (address + opt-out). Honor any opt-out immediately.
  • In Acquisition outreach, the buyer/equity group is never named.
