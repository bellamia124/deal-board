The flow — five steps, every firm

1

Sign in

Your work email + your password. First time: click Set my password. Only the three of us have access.

2

Claim it

Assign a firm to yourself before you dial. If it shows another name, it's taken — leave it. No double-dialing.

3

Read the card

Check priority, the "ask for" name, and whether it's a seller or a competitor (see below).

4

Make the call

Work your state/region group in the best windows. Describe the buyer — never name them.

5

Log & schedule

Hit the outcome button and set the Next date + note so the follow-up doesn't get lost.

Logging your action

Called / No answerStandard call outcomes. "Call back" keeps it in your queue.
EmailedLog it when you hand-email a firm. This pulls that firm out of the automated email sequence so they never get both.
AUTO OFFAppears once a firm is emailed or replies — automation is now off for them. A human owns the thread.
Not for sale / Do not callCloses the card and stops all outreach. Use "Do not call" the moment they ask.

Owner-operators answer their own phone before and after the crew's day.
Only firms with a phone or email show. Firms with no contact yet are hidden so the list stays a real call list — type a name in search to pull one up anyway. Log every reply to the board the same day.

Rules that don't bend

  • Never name the buyer in outreach — describe the profile; the name is disclosed 1:1, under NDA.
  • Never quote the revenue estimate on a call — it's a model number, not a fact.
  • Assign before you dial — one caller per firm, always.
  • Hand-email = log "Emailed" — that's what turns their automation off.
  • ALREADY ACQUIRED = competitor, not a lead — skip it.
  • Log every reply same day so the whole team sees it.
